In episode 23 of the Togo AI Lab broadcast, on automation and agentic AI, I explained how to automate a data pipeline and hand it to a team that does not code. The case study was the PCARRS accelerometer pipeline built at CCDC, where R and Python each do the part they do best and the field team only sees a desktop application and a report. The closing line: nobody will pay you for your language, they will pay you for the problem you solve.

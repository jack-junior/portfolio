At TAISS 2026 (University of Lomé and Togo AI Lab, 24–29 August, 100 participants) I presented my work on missing data with a simple question: you train a model on field data, part of it is missing, can you trust it? The week covered deep learning, convolutional networks, retrieval-augmented generation and LLMs.

title: "Digital and financial access in Togo"
summary: "Where can people in Togo reach a telecom agency or a mobile-money agent? Using Geodata Togo (19,788 mobile-money agents) and the 2022 census (39 prefectures, 396 cantons), I mapped access and built interactive dashboards. Twelve of the 39 prefectures, home to 17.9% of the population, have no telecom agency. The median distance to a mobile-money service point is 2.0 km, against 16.2 km to an operator agency. The largest gap is peri-urban: Agoè-Nyivé, about 883,000 people."

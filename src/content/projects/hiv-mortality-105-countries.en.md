summary: "An ecological analysis of 2024 UNAIDS and World Bank data for 105 countries with established epidemics. National wealth correlates with HIV mortality but explains less than half of the variation between countries; robust regression residuals point to countries that do better than their income predicts. In African countries, the median ratio of AIDS deaths to people living with HIV is 2.27% for men against 1.25% for women."
